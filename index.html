1. Introduction

Welcome to Life Clock ("the App"), developed by MD Ismail and published by Cybro Technology ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled entirely locally on your Android device.

2. Data We Process & Local Storage

To calculate live life statistics, age milestones, and countdown notifications, the App processes user-supplied details locally:

  • Personal Details: Your Name, Date of Birth, and Time of Birth.
  • Demographic Metrics: Country of residence and estimated life expectancy.
  • Loved Ones & Family: Names, relationships, and birthdates you choose to add.
  • Profile Pictures: Images you pick from your device's photo gallery.

Complete Local Isolation: All data is stored in a private Room SQLite database on your device. Selected photos are compressed and stored strictly inside the App's sandboxed storage directory.

3. Permissions & System Access

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Used solely to deliver daily motivational quotes and scheduled birthday reminders.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Re-arms your scheduled notification alarms and updates the home-screen widget after your phone restarts.
  • VIBRATE: Provides gentle tactile feedback when alerts arrive.
  • Zero-Permission Photo Selection: We utilize Android’s official Photo Picker, which does not require any storage read permissions.

4. Zero Third-Party Sharing & No Ads

Cybro Technology does not embed any advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or third-party analytics. We do not collect device advertising identifiers (GAID) or sell data to anyone.

5. Complete User Control & Data Deletion

  • You can edit or delete individual records at any time within the App.
  • You can clear all stored records with one tap via Settings > Reset All Data.
  • Uninstalling the App instantly and irreversibly removes all data from your device.
